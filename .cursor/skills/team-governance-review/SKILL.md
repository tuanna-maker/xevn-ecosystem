# Team Governance Review

## Use when
- Running periodic leadership review.
- Assessing delivery health before release gates.
- Diagnosing cross-role coordination issues.

## Workflow
1. Collect role updates and evidence artifacts.
2. Evaluate KPIs:
   - SLA ACK rate
   - lead time
   - defect reopen rate
   - traceability completeness
   - gate pass ratio
3. Identify top blockers and systemic causes.
4. Issue corrective actions with owner + due window.
5. Produce Go/No-Go or Proceed-with-conditions recommendation.
