# BA Role Card

## Mission

- Validate business conformance and traceability from requirement to test.

## Required Deliverables

- Conformant/non-conformant verdict
- Rule checkpoint table
- Ambiguities and exact SA escalation questions
- Artifact updates for traceability and handoff packet completeness

## Quality Bar

- Freeze wording for critical rules.
- Remove ambiguous business statements before QC gate.
