## 2. Architecture verdict (normative)

### 2.1 Dual SM lock — cycle ≠ eval → **ACK**

| Axis | Persist | Enum / transition | SA lock |
|------|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Cycle** | `performance_cycles.status` | `draft \| active \| closed` | SRS wording **`open` ≡ `active`** — **cấm** DDL-rename to `open` |
| Cycle cancel | same | `draft → closed` **OK** (cancel without activate) | ACK DB map |
| **Evaluation** | `performance_evaluations.status` (**ADD**) | `draft → submitted → approved → completed` | No jump; withdraw submitted→draft **default ✗** |
| Labels U72 | FE dictionary | Nháp · Đã nộp · Đã duyệt · Hoàn thành | **Cấm** raw enum on badge |
| **Policy** | `hrm_insurance_policies.status` | `draft → active → expired \| cancelled` | Labels: Nháp · Đang hiệu lực · Hết hạn · Đã hủy |

**Orthogonal rule:** **Cấm** collapse cycle enums into eval 4-state (or vice versa). Helper domain keys: `performance_cycle` · `performance_evaluation` · `insurance_policy` · `insurance_record` · `leave` · `recruitment`.
